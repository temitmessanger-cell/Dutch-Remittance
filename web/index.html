Dutch Remit

Dutch Remit is Cameroon's biggest cross-border remittance and virtual card platform, and one of Africa's leading platforms — connecting Cameroon and the wider African diaspora with money transfer and card services across Europe, the UAE and Asia, with coverage expanding.

Users can send money, manage transfers, track payments, and access financial services through a secure digital experience designed for international transfers and account-based payments — with real, live exchange rates, mobile money and bank payouts across 120+ countries and 20+ currencies, backed by global fintech infrastructure.

  • Cameroon's biggest cross-border remittance and virtual card platform
  • International money transfer and card services across 120+ countries
  • 20+ currencies, including local money like XAF, NGN and GHS
  • Cross-border payments reaching Europe, the UAE and Asia
  • Secure remittance and transfer experience for the African diaspora
