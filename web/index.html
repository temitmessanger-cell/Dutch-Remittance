Dutch Remit

Dutch Remit is a cross-border money transfer and international remittance platform connecting African communities with global payment services.

Users can send money, manage transfers, track payments, and access financial services through a secure digital experience designed for international transfers and account-based payments.

  • International money transfer services
  • Cross-border payments across multiple countries
  • Secure remittance and transfer experience
